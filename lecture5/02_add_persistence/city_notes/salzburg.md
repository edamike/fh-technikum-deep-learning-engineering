# Salzburg, Austria

## Transit
The Salzburg Card (24h €30 / 48h €39) covers unlimited public transport plus entry to 30+ attractions including the Hohensalzburg funicular. Worth it if you plan to visit more than three paid sights.

## Must-see
Hohensalzburg Fortress is the most-visited landmark in Austria outside Vienna. Walk up Festungsgasse (free, ~15 minutes) or take the funicular (€16 return).

## Free attractions
Mirabell Gardens are open daily until dusk, free entry. The Kapitelplatz chessboard and the Petersfriedhof cemetery (one of Europe's oldest) are also free to visit.

## Cheap eats
Balkan Grill on Getreidegasse serves the Bosna sausage sandwich (invented in Salzburg, €5). Augustiner Bräu is a monastery beer garden where you bring your own food from adjacent stalls.

## Student tips
Mozart's Birthplace and the Residenz offer student tickets around €9. The Sound of Music tour has an evening Sparangebot at ~€35.

## Rainy day and indoor options
On rainy days visit the DomQuartier (cathedral + Residenz + museum complex, all connected under cover). In summer heat, Hellbrunn Palace water gardens offer trick fountains and shade, a 30-minute bus ride from the centre.

## Day trips
Hallstatt is 80 minutes away by train and ferry. Werfen (ice cave) is 45 minutes by train; the cable car + cave tour combo takes a half-day.
