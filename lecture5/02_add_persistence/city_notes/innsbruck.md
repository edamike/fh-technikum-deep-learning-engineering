# Innsbruck, Austria

## Transit
The Innsbruck Card (24h €53 / 48h €63) includes the Nordkettenbahn cable car, Hungerburgbahn funicular, and all public transport. Without the card, the Nordkette return trip is €41.

## Must-see
From the city centre, you can be at 2,256m in under 20 minutes via the Hungerburgbahn + Nordkettenbahn combo. The top station has panoramic views over the Inn Valley and the Zillertal Alps.

## Free attractions
The Goldenes Dachl (Golden Roof) balcony is free to view from the street. The Hofkirche courtyard and most of the Altstadt pedestrian zone are free to wander.

## Cheap eats
The Markthalle at Innrain hosts dozens of food stalls with lunch deals around €10. Students eat at Mensa Technikerstraße for €6-8.

## Rainy day and indoor options
In rain or cold, Swarovski Crystal Worlds (Wattens, 20 min by shuttle from the main station) is mostly indoor. The Ferdinandeum art museum and the Zeughaus arms museum are both covered and in the city centre.

## Ski access
In winter, the lift ticket for Nordkette is €46/day. Ski and snowboard rental on-mountain starts at €35/day. The Stubai Glacier (35 min by bus) stays open into June.

## Day trips
The Stams Abbey is a 40-minute train ride, with Cistercian baroque architecture. Seefeld, a cross-country ski resort, is 30 minutes by train.
