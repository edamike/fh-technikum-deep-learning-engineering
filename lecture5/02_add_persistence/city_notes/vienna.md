# Vienna, Austria

## Transit
Vienna's U-Bahn runs until about midnight on weekdays and 24 hours on Friday and Saturday nights. A 72-hour Vienna Pass covers most tourist attractions and public transport.

## Free attractions
Many national museums (Albertina, MAK, Kunsthistorisches, Natural History) offer free entry on the first Sunday of each month. The MuseumsQuartier courtyards are free to enter anytime and host pop-up events in summer.

## Cheap eats
The Naschmarkt (Mon-Sat) has affordable Turkish and Vienna food stalls. Würstelstände sell Käsekrainer and Bosna for under €6. Try Bitzinger behind the Albertina for the classic experience.

## Student tips
Most museums offer 50% student discounts with an ID card. The Prater amusement park is free to enter — you only pay per ride, and the public walking paths through the whole park are free.

## Rainy day and indoor options
When it's rainy or cold, head underground: the U-Bahn connects most central attractions, and the Albertina, Kunsthistorisches and Natural History museums are all covered. In summer heat, the Donauinsel offers free swimming spots and shade along the Danube.

## Nightlife
The Bermuda Triangle near Stephansplatz has dense bars for a short pub crawl. For something quieter, the vineyards (Heurige) in Grinzing and Nussdorf serve young wine; the 38A bus from Heiligenstadt U-Bahn reaches them.
