# Graz, Austria

## Transit
Trams are free inside the inner city (the Altstadt / Hauptplatz zone). A GrazMobil city bike costs €1 per 30 minutes with the first 15 minutes free, and stations are dense in the centre.

## Must-see
The Schlossberg clock tower offers the best old-town view. Walk up (free) or take the Schlossbergbahn funicular (€2.50) or the elevator inside the mountain (€1.80).

## Free attractions
The Kunsthaus Graz has free entry on Wednesdays after 4pm. The Herz-Jesu-Kirche and the Mausoleum next to the Cathedral are free to visit any day.

## Cheap eats
Studentenfutter at the Uni-Mensa (around Universitätsplatz) costs €5-8. The Bauernmarkt at Kaiser-Josef-Platz (Mon-Sat) has cheap fresh produce and bakery stalls.

## Rainy day and indoor options
When it's rainy, visit the Landeszeughaus (historical armoury, €11) or move between the underground gallery spaces on Sackstraße. On hot days, the Augarten pond and Stadtpark are shaded and have ice cream kiosks.

## Day trips
The Lipizzaner stud farm in Piber is a 45-minute bus ride; tours run twice a day in summer. Vienna is a 2.5-hour direct train ride, which also makes Graz a useful base for a wider Styria trip.
